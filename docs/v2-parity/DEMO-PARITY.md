# MedusaPOS against demo.wcpos.com/pos, screen by screen

- **Date:** 2026-11-10
- **Demo:** https://demo.wcpos.com/pos (WCPOS 2.0.0, signed in with the published demo / demo)
- **MedusaPOS:** `2ebcb77` (`apps/expo` web export, e2e store: 5 E2E products, EUR, Europe region with 25% VAT)
- **Viewports:** desktop 1280×800; phone 390×844 for the 17 rows (round 2).
- **Rounds:** round 1 shot rows 01–06c and the demo side. Round 2, the same day on the same MedusaPOS code, shot the MedusaPOS side of 07, 09c–09f, 10, 13, 13b, 15a–15g and 17a–17c, plus the demo phone POS and menu.
- **Rubric:**
  - `match`: a cashier would not notice a difference.
  - `close`: same job and same place, with visibly different controls or density.
  - `gap`: missing, or done in a way a cashier trained on WCPOS would stumble over.
  - `N/A`: WooCommerce or WordPress only; the row names Medusa's own way.
- **Score: 0 / 44 match** (21 close, 23 gap; 5 N/A rows are excluded).

## How the shots were taken

The demo was used read-only.

- A Playwright route guard aborted:
  - every non-GET request except the WordPress sign-in form post;
  - every host outside demo/docs.wcpos.com.
- The guard did allow GETs of the app bundle from `cdn.wcpos.com` and `cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/wcpos/`, because without them the app stays on its splash screen.
- The demo app's own background POSTs were all aborted. These were `wp-json/wcpos/v2/push/orders`, updates.wcpos.com, api.notifications.wcpos.com and the beacon.
- Choosing an already-open register only binds the browser locally.
- States that need a write were not taken from the demo. Instead:
  - they use the docs.wcpos.com screenshot, marked "demo: docs <url>";
  - or, where an open cart already existed on the demo, it was viewed but not changed.

Catalogue size is ignored throughout.

Owners:
- `app`: `apps/expo`
- `TU <package> <component>`: TallyUI
- `plugin`: `packages/medusa-plugin`

D = `https://docs.wcpos.com/img/2.x`

## Top 15 gaps a cashier notices first

Round 2 added two rows to this list: the paid screen (07, now 3) and Orders (10, now 10). They pushed out round 1's 14 (no Products screen) and 15 (no product display or cart settings). Both are still gaps in the checklist (12, 02d, 05d).

1. Cart lines are tall blocks with −/+/Remove buttons, not the compact QTY / ITEM / PRICE / TOTAL table (`TU components Cart`).
2. No full tender pane with a keypad; payment is a small form in the cart column behind Cash / Card buttons. On a phone the tender does not even show the amount to pay (`TU components Tender`, `TU components CashTendered`).
3. The paid screen shows change as one small row, not a large headline. It has no Email Receipt or Download PDF and drops the order summary (`TU components Receipt`, `app`).
4. No open-orders tab strip; parked sales hide in a modal and need an explicit "Park this sale" (`TU components Cart`, `app`).
5. No navigation rail (desktop) or menu drawer and bottom tabs (phone); a text header with only Orders, Reports, Settings and Sign out (`app`).
6. Product tiles have no images (`TU components ProductCard`, `app`).
7. No single "Checkout €total" button (`TU components Cart`).
8. Qty and price edits use an inline form, not a keypad popover with quick discount keys (`TU components Cart`, `TU components QuantityStepper`).
9. No cart "+" menu; no miscellaneous (custom) product line (`TU components Cart`, `plugin`).
10. Orders is a short "Recent" list with no search, filters, status, cashier or payment method (`app`, `TU components OrderList`).
11. No Customers screen (`app`).
12. No coupon or promotion-code entry (`app`, `plugin`).
13. No avatar or user sheet with sales today, drawer amount, hand the till over, switch store or register (`app`, `TU components RegisterPanel`).

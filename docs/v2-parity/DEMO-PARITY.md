# MedusaPOS against demo.wcpos.com/pos, screen by screen

- **Date:** 2026-11-16 (re-rated; the first rating was 2026-11-10 on `2ebcb77`)
- **Demo:** https://demo.wcpos.com/pos (WCPOS 2.0.0, signed in with the published demo / demo)
- **MedusaPOS:** `359d8d7` on `main`, `@tallyui/*` 3.20.1 (`apps/expo` web export, e2e store: 5 E2E products, EUR, Europe region with 25% VAT)
- **Viewports:** desktop 1280×800; phone 390×844 for the 17 rows.
- **Rounds:** the demo side is unchanged from 2026-11-10 (rounds 1 and 2). On 2026-11-16 every MedusaPOS shot was taken again on `359d8d7` and every row re-rated.
- **Rubric:**
  - `match`: a cashier would not notice a difference.
  - `close`: same job and same place, with visibly different controls or density.
  - `gap`: missing, or done in a way a cashier trained on WCPOS would stumble over.
  - `N/A`: WooCommerce or WordPress only; the row names Medusa's own way.
- **Score: 1 / 44 match** (29 close, 14 gap; 5 N/A rows are excluded). On 2026-11-10 it was 0 / 44 (21 close, 23 gap).

## How the shots were taken

The demo was used read-only.

- A Playwright route guard aborted:
  - every non-GET request except the WordPress sign-in form post;
  - every host outside demo/docs.wcpos.com.
- The guard did allow GETs of the app bundle from `cdn.wcpos.com` and `cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/wcpos/`, because without them the app stays on its splash screen.
- The demo app's own background POSTs were all aborted. These were `wp-json/wcpos/v2/push/orders`, updates.wcpos.com, api.notifications.wcpos.com and the beacon.
- Choosing an already-open register only binds the browser locally.
- States that need a write were not taken from the demo. Instead:
  - they use the docs.wcpos.com screenshot, marked "demo: docs <url>";
  - or, where an open cart already existed on the demo, it was viewed but not changed.

The MedusaPOS side was driven by a throwaway Playwright spec against the local e2e store (6 tests, one per journey, all passing). E2E-1 stock was not sold.

Catalogue size is ignored throughout.

Owners:
- `app`: `apps/expo`
- `TU <package> <component>`: TallyUI
- `plugin`: `packages/medusa-plugin`

D = `https://docs.wcpos.com/img/2.x`

## Top 15 gaps a cashier notices first

Since 2026-11-10 the cart table, a single Checkout button, the keypad popovers, the tender keypad and tiles, the icon rail and phone drawer, coupon codes and themes have landed. They left this list. Products and display settings (12, 02d, 05d) re-entered it.

1. The paid screen is a narrow receipt column: change is one small row, there is no order summary beside it and no Email Receipt or Download PDF, although the plugin's receipt-email route exists (`app`, `TU components Receipt`, `TU connector-medusa`).
2. No open-orders tab strip; parked sales hide in a modal and need an explicit "Park this sale" (`app`, TU `OpenSaleTabs` is unused).
3. Orders is a short "Recent" list of this till's sales: no search, filters, status, cashier or payment method, and a row does not open (`app`, `plugin` order query).
4. No refunds from an order (`TU pos` `submitOrderRefund` is unused, `plugin`, `app`).
5. Reports is three rows of today's figures, with no chart, cards, period or register scope (`app`; TU `SalesRoom` is unused).
6. No cart "+" menu; miscellaneous product hides under "Add charge" as "Custom item", and "⋮" holds only Void (`TU components Cart`, `app`).
7. Product tiles show no images on this catalogue, and there are no filter pills or scan / settings icons beside search (`TU components ProductCard`, `app`).
8. The navigation rail has four areas; no Products, Customers or Coupons, and the selling screen is titled "Products" (`app`).
9. No avatar or user sheet with sales today, drawer amount, hand-over or switch store / register (`app`; TU `HandoverSlip`, `SwitchConfirm`).
10. No Customers screen (`app`).
11. Settings is one long page with no sub-nav, language or default customer (`app`; TU `SettingsLayout`).
12. No product or cart display settings (tile size, sort, cart side, quick discount keys) (`app`).
13. The tender sits in the cart column with the products still live beside it, and takes two presses ("Take…", "Complete sale") (`app`, `TU components Tender`).
14. Line discounts open an inline form and show as a red chip, with no quick −5/−10/−15% keys and no struck-through price (`TU components Cart`, `app`).
15. The register panel is a centred modal without "open since", drawer headline or X report, and pay in / out stacks a second modal (`app`; TU `RegisterPanel` `presentation='side'`, `onPrintXReport`).
